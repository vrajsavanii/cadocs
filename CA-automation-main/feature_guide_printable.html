1. Executive Summary: Why Indian CAs Need This

Most Chartered Accountants in India waste over 60% of their billable hours on non-billable administrative friction:

  • Chasing reluctant clients over phone and WhatsApp for bank statements, GST invoices, and Form 16/26AS.
  • Tracking dozens of statutory deadlines across GST, TDS, Advance Tax, and ROC on scattered Excel files.
  • Answering repetitive, routine tax questions late at night when the partner wants to rest.
  • Losing cash flow because audit and filing fee reminders are awkward to send manually.
The Transformation: Digital Practice Operating System

This software centralizes all client records into a familiar Google Sheets cloud database, deploys an AI client support desk, automates morning WhatsApp reminders on statutory schedules, and tracks every rupee of billing recovery with zero manual effort.
